Procurement Approval Engine & Delivery Ownership

Scope ERP Delivery Management
Procurement & Approvals
Tech Stack Odoo 18 & 19
Purchase, Inventory,
Accounting, Approvals
PostgreSQL
Role Odoo Techno Functional Consultant
Delivery Owner
Duration Ongoing

Business Challenge

  • A multi-company group needed one procurement process spanning several branches and regions, each with its own authority limits.
  • Approval rules lived in people’s heads and in spreadsheets, so the same request was routed differently depending on who handled it.
  • Local and foreign purchasing followed genuinely different flows, but the system treated them identically.
  • Goods receipts were accepted against purchase orders with no tolerance checking, so over- and under-delivery went unnoticed until reconciliation.
  • Role boundaries were unclear: users could see and act on documents belonging to other companies.

Solution Delivered

  • Ran discovery with procurement, finance and branch stakeholders and turned the outcome into written functional specs with explicit acceptance criteria.
  • Built a configurable approval engine: workflow definitions scoped by a domain, so one model can carry different approval chains for different record types — no per-flow code.
  • Modelled the full request chain from material request through purchase request to purchase order, with defined rules for when approval is required and when it is safely skipped.
  • Separated local from foreign procurement: direct purchase orders with splitting for local, multi-quotation comparison for foreign, plus budget checks on the foreign side.
  • Scoped approvers, notifications and pending-action lists to the requesting company, with a documented fallback so nothing silently loses its approver.
  • Added a goods-receipt tolerance check comparing demand against received quantity within a configurable percentage band.
  • Defined four procurement roles with access rights mapped per document type, and put a regression test gate in CI to protect the workflow rules.

Delivery Practice

  • Requirements captured as written specs and confirmed with stakeholders before any development started.
  • Every change tested functionally on a staging database and signed off before reaching production users.
  • Version upgrades batched deliberately rather than applied per change, so production upgrades stay predictable.
  • Workflow rules and naming conventions documented so a future developer inherits the reasoning, not just the code.
  • Delivered fully remote and async, across time zones, with written updates as the default channel.

Odoo Amazon FBA Connector

Scope Integration & Automation
Tech Stack Odoo 18
Amazon SP-API
Odoo SH
Role Odoo Developer
Odoo Functional Consultant
Duration 6+ Months

Business Challenge

  • Amazon FBA operations were distributed across multiple tools and platforms.
  • Invoicing and financial records were managed in Xero, disconnected from Odoo.
  • Inventory tracking and demand forecasting were maintained manually using Google Sheets.
  • Lack of a single source of truth caused stock inconsistencies between systems.
  • Manual data entry increased operational overhead and risk of human error.
  • Delayed visibility into orders, inventory levels, and financial data impacted decision-making.

Solution Delivered

  • Designed and implemented a centralized Amazon FBA integration within Odoo.
  • Automated synchronization of inventory levels between Amazon FBA and Odoo.
  • Streamlined order imports with accurate fulfillment and shipment status updates.
  • Enabled structured data flow to support accounting and financial reconciliation.
  • Replaced manual Google Sheets tracking with real-time inventory visibility inside Odoo.
  • Implemented validation and error-handling mechanisms to ensure data consistency.

Business Impact

  • Established Odoo as the single source of truth for Amazon FBA operations.
  • Eliminated manual reconciliation between Xero, Google Sheets, and Amazon.
  • Significantly reduced stock mismatches and inventory discrepancies.
  • Improved operational efficiency by minimizing manual data entry.
  • Enabled real-time visibility into inventory, orders, and fulfillment status.
  • Provided reliable data for forecasting, reporting, and business decision-making.
