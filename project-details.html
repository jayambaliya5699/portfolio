Workflow Studio — Approval & Access Platform

Scope Platform Engineering
Approvals & Access Control
Tech Stack Odoo 18
base.automation
Record Rules
PostgreSQL
Role Techno Functional Consultant
Solution Design
Duration Ongoing

Business Challenge

  • Approval logic had been written separately for every document type — sales, purchase, invoicing, stock — each as its own module with its own quirks.
  • Changing an approval rule meant a developer, a code change and a release, so business users could not adjust their own authority limits.
  • The same conceptual rule was implemented inconsistently across modules, and the differences were invisible until something was approved by the wrong person.
  • There was no single audit answer to “who approved this, and under which rule?”
  • Access rights were scattered across groups with no readable model of who could do what.

Solution Delivered

  • Designed a single approval engine — approval types, rules, requests and request lines — that any model can use without writing model-specific code.
  • Workflow definitions are scoped by a domain, so one model can carry different approval chains for different record types. Local and foreign purchase orders, for example, route differently off the same mechanism.
  • Approvals fire on any model through base automations rather than a mixin, so onboarding a new document type is configuration, not development.
  • Built a role-based access layer structured as Roles → Permissions → Privileges, so access can be reasoned about and reviewed in business language.
  • Added an audit log and reason-capture mixin covering approvals, rejections and closures.
  • Handled the awkward edges properly: approval types on company-less models, and approver resolution scoped to the requesting company with a documented fallback.

Migration & Impact

  • Wrote a bridge module reproducing the legacy per-document approval flows on top of the new engine, so the old modules could be retired without a big-bang cutover.
  • Legacy approval modules were emptied one at a time behind the bridge rather than deleted underneath live users.
  • Business users can now define and change approval chains themselves; developer involvement is no longer on the critical path for a policy change.
  • One consistent audit trail across every approved document type.
  • The engine was later backported to a second Odoo 18 codebase and reused on a third deployment — it became a product rather than a customisation.

Manufacturing & PLM Implementation

Scope Manufacturing, MRP
PLM & Quality
Tech Stack Odoo 18
MRP, PLM, Quality
Sales & Inventory
XLSX Reporting
Role Odoo Developer →
Techno Functional Consultant
Duration Multi-year · 1,250+ commits

Business Challenge

  • Engineered-to-order products meant nearly every sales order implied a new or modified bill of materials, and building them by hand was slow and error-prone.
  • Product variants carried many configurable attributes, and mismatches between what was sold and what was planned surfaced late — often on the shop floor.
  • Costing was incomplete: BOMs did not carry additional costs or supplier discounts, so margin at quotation time was unreliable.
  • Engineering needed BOM data in spreadsheets for review, and production needed quality-check results in a reportable form.
  • Planners had no consolidated view of which approved deliveries were actually ready for production.

Solution Delivered

  • Built a guided BOM creation and customisation layer on top of Odoo MRP, driven by product variant options, so configured products generate their BOM rather than having one typed in.
  • Extended BOM costing with additional costs and discount handling, feeding accurate figures back into sale margin.
  • Linked sales orders to PLM: BOM-driven order lines, sale-to-PLM approval flow, and BOM reporting from the sales side.
  • Delivered BOM Excel/PLM reports generated straight from the product, plus low-stock visibility against BOM components.
  • Built manufacturing quality-check reporting on top of Odoo’s quality module for production orders.
  • Created a bulk planning dashboard showing deliveries approved for production, with aging, work-centre and MO status views — including automatic detection of attribute mismatches between sold and planned configurations.
  • Added a sales order tracking dashboard covering production, delivery and invoice status in one place.

Business Impact

  • Configured products move from quotation to a costed, buildable BOM without manual re-entry.
  • Attribute mismatches are caught at planning time instead of on the shop floor.
  • Quotation margins reflect real BOM cost including extras and discounts.
  • Engineering, planning and sales work from the same BOM data rather than parallel spreadsheets.
  • Planners get one screen answering “what can we actually build next?”

Procurement Approval Engine & Delivery Ownership

Scope ERP Delivery Management
Procurement & Approvals
Tech Stack Odoo 18 & 19
Purchase, Inventory,
Accounting, Approvals
PostgreSQL
Role Odoo Techno Functional Consultant
Delivery Owner
Duration Ongoing

Business Challenge

  • A multi-company group needed one procurement process spanning several branches and regions, each with its own authority limits.
  • Approval rules lived in people’s heads and in spreadsheets, so the same request was routed differently depending on who handled it.
  • Local and foreign purchasing followed genuinely different flows, but the system treated them identically.
  • Goods receipts were accepted against purchase orders with no tolerance checking, so over- and under-delivery went unnoticed until reconciliation.
  • Role boundaries were unclear: users could see and act on documents belonging to other companies.

Solution Delivered

  • Ran discovery with procurement, finance and branch stakeholders and turned the outcome into written functional specs with explicit acceptance criteria.
  • Built a configurable approval engine: workflow definitions scoped by a domain, so one model can carry different approval chains for different record types — no per-flow code.
  • Modelled the full request chain from material request through purchase request to purchase order, with defined rules for when approval is required and when it is safely skipped.
  • Separated local from foreign procurement: direct purchase orders with splitting for local, multi-quotation comparison for foreign, plus budget checks on the foreign side.
  • Scoped approvers, notifications and pending-action lists to the requesting company, with a documented fallback so nothing silently loses its approver.
  • Added a goods-receipt tolerance check comparing demand against received quantity within a configurable percentage band.
  • Defined four procurement roles with access rights mapped per document type, and put a regression test gate in CI to protect the workflow rules.

Delivery Practice

  • Requirements captured as written specs and confirmed with stakeholders before any development started.
  • Every change tested functionally on a staging database and signed off before reaching production users.
  • Version upgrades batched deliberately rather than applied per change, so production upgrades stay predictable.
  • Workflow rules and naming conventions documented so a future developer inherits the reasoning, not just the code.
  • Delivered fully remote and async, across time zones, with written updates as the default channel.

Odoo Amazon FBA Connector

Scope Integration & Automation
Tech Stack Odoo 18
Amazon SP-API
Odoo SH
Role Odoo Developer
Odoo Functional Consultant
Duration 6+ Months

Business Challenge

  • Amazon FBA operations were distributed across multiple tools and platforms.
  • Invoicing and financial records were managed in Xero, disconnected from Odoo.
  • Inventory tracking and demand forecasting were maintained manually using Google Sheets.
  • Lack of a single source of truth caused stock inconsistencies between systems.
  • Manual data entry increased operational overhead and risk of human error.
  • Delayed visibility into orders, inventory levels, and financial data impacted decision-making.

Solution Delivered

  • Designed and implemented a centralized Amazon FBA integration within Odoo.
  • Automated synchronization of inventory levels between Amazon FBA and Odoo.
  • Streamlined order imports with accurate fulfillment and shipment status updates.
  • Enabled structured data flow to support accounting and financial reconciliation.
  • Replaced manual Google Sheets tracking with real-time inventory visibility inside Odoo.
  • Implemented validation and error-handling mechanisms to ensure data consistency.

Business Impact

  • Established Odoo as the single source of truth for Amazon FBA operations.
  • Eliminated manual reconciliation between Xero, Google Sheets, and Amazon.
  • Significantly reduced stock mismatches and inventory discrepancies.
  • Improved operational efficiency by minimizing manual data entry.
  • Enabled real-time visibility into inventory, orders, and fulfillment status.
  • Provided reliable data for forecasting, reporting, and business decision-making.
